How are two groups, people, companies or tools linked? The most specific chains of stated links between them – not only the shortest – with the evidence of every step.

How chains are chosen

A step costs {{ base.us }} on an official sanctions list, {{ base.wikidata }} in reference data (Wikidata, Wikipedia, ATT&CK) and {{ base.reports }} where two actors are only named together in reports. Passing through an entity adds {{ hub }} for every doubling of its links – a chain through Islamic State or the “Donetsk People's Republic” (hundreds of links) says little, one through a small front company says much – and passing through a common system tool (net, PowerShell, Mimikatz) adds 1.5 more. Each step reads as the source states it: “← linked to” means the next entity is linked to the previous one. The cheapest chain comes first. The next chains may not reuse its intermediate entities, so they are other routes, not variations of one. At most {{ max_steps }} steps; countries are left out unless asked for. A chain is a set of stated links, not a finding: read the evidence. Entries of different sanctions lists are not merged yet, so one company can appear as several entities (choose the one with more links).
