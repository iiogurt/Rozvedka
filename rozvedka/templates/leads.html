How leads are found

The stated network is every layer of the Network except co-mentions in reports. Two actors (not countries) are a lead when they share at least {{ min_shared }} neighbours there, are not linked to each other in any layer and are never named within one passage of any report. Each shared neighbour counts 1 ÷ log(its number of links) (Adamic–Adar): sharing a small front company says more than sharing a hub. Neighbours with more than {{ hub }} links, and shared malware, tools and campaigns (see Toolkit twins), are not counted, nor links saying two are rivals – sharing an enemy is no sign of a connection. Many leads are a lineage the sources describe in other words (a predecessor organisation, a parallel unit) – mark them; a review is kept with its note in sources/lead_reviews.yaml.
