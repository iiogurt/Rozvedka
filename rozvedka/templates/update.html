Update – check the agencies for new reports

Rozvedka looks for new reports only when you start it here (there is no schedule). An update visits the report pages of the agencies in the registry, lists the reports it has not seen before, downloads them and indexes them (text, topics, dates, actors). Sources that block automatic access are collected by hand on To collect.
