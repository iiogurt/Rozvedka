One entity in the middle, its stated links on the inner ring and the entities two steps away on the outer ring – a sector for each source, every name readable.

How this is drawn

The inner ring shows at most {{ inner }} direct links. Neighbours stated by most sources come first, then actors the reports name, then the most specific ones (fewest links of their own). Each sits in the sector of the strongest source that states its link: official lists before reference data before co-mentions. A double line means several sources state it. The outer ring shows at most {{ outer }} entities linked to the neighbours shown. Those linked to several of them come first, and hubs are weighed down ({{ hub }} for every doubling of their links, as on Find a connection). Each sits beside the neighbours it is linked to. Countries are left out unless asked for: nearly everything is linked to Russia or Ukraine somewhere. Every sector says how many links it has and how many are not drawn; each count opens the list of links with their evidence. Two steps away is not a link: open the chain on Find a connection.
