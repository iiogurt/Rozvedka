Offshore leaks leaked records · not official

Companies and people of the sanctions lists that ICIJ's Offshore Leaks database names – the leaked records of offshore service providers behind the Panama, Paradise and Pandora Papers – and the officers, shareholders and intermediaries linked to them.

These are leaked documents published by journalists, not an official register: often years old, incomplete, and a name in them is no evidence of wrongdoing. Every link from them is marked icij and drawn in orange, dashed.

How this is matched

A list entry is the ICIJ record with the same name (case, accents and punctuation ignored) in the same country. Companies: distinctive names only (two words beyond the legal form, or one of 8 letters or more), and every ICIJ record with that name must be in the entry's country – they become links at once. People: a name of two words and {{ min_person }} letters or more, carried by at most {{ max_people }} ICIJ records, all in the entry's country. That is still only the same name and country – many people share one – so a person's match becomes a link only after you confirm it below (kept in sources/icij_reviews.yaml), and the network is rebuilt with build-graph or the next Update. One step out: the companies they are officers, shareholders or beneficial owners of, the officers of their companies, and their intermediaries; registered addresses and ICIJ's own "same name" guesses are left out.
