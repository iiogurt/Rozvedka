How democratic was a state when its agencies wrote a report? Every value opens its source.

How to read it

A threat assessment from an electoral autocracy, or from a democracy that has since slid, reads differently from one by a stable liberal democracy. Each row shows the state's regime type year by year since 1990 (colour) with V-Dem's Liberal Democracy Index as a line, and the latest value of three independent measurements. Report pages show the ratings of the report's own year.

How to read it

Each column is one measurement with its own source, scale and direction – they are not combined into a score. The small line is the state's course since 1990 on the measure's scale; the number is its latest value and year, then its place among the library's states (the better end first). The state's name opens all of its ratings.
