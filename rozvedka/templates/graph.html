How this is built

Every link is something a source states; nothing is inferred. Official lists: OFAC's “Linked To” remarks (the name resolved on the same list), and the narratives of UK, UN and EU listings that name another listing of the same list by its full name (one-word names only for organisations and only where written as a name; names made only of generic words such as “Ministry of Defence” are not looked for) – the sentence is kept as evidence. Two samples of 40 such links read by hand: 37 and 38 right, the wrong ones led to the rules above. Reference data: Wikidata statements and Wikipedia infobox fields of the actor gazetteer; MITRE ATT&CK's groups, software and campaigns. Reports: two actors (not countries) named in one passage of at least two reports – a co-mention, not a stated tie. A list entry that the sanctions matching tied to an actor by its exact name is that actor, so layers meet there; entries of different lists are not yet merged with each other.
