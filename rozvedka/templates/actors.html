Where the actors come from

  • Who is listed: Wikidata items of selected classes (intelligence agencies, hacker groups, private military companies, paramilitary and terrorist organisations, organised crime groups, cartels), items designated as terrorist (Wikidata property P3461), MITRE ATT&CK threat groups, and actors added by hand in sources/actors.yaml. Each actor page says why the actor is listed.
  • How mentions are found: by name. A report mentions an actor when one of the actor's names – Wikidata labels and aliases in the report languages, MITRE ATT&CK aliases, hand-added names – occurs in its text. Names likely to mean something else are dropped; each actor page lists its names with the reason any of them is not used.
  • Counts are reports (documents), not mentions; the same filters as the Documents page apply.
