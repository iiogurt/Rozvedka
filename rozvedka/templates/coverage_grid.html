Coverage grid

Every state in scope against the national agency types: covered, ∅ checked – no regular reports published, or open – still to research.

How to read it

A covered cell counts the state's agencies of that type in the registry and their listed reports (one file per report; the number opens them), with the newest report's year – grey when older than {{ g.stale }} years, ✋ where the site is collected by hand. 0 means the source is listed but has no report yet: a source to fix. ∅ means the slot was researched and no regular public report found – the date and what was found are in the cell's tooltip and in {{ g.file }}. An empty cell is open: nobody has looked yet, or nothing was recorded. Other official bodies and the state's think tanks ◆ and commercial analysts ■ are counted apart, never in the agency types. EU bodies, NATO and international organisations are on Agencies.
