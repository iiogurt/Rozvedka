How this is drawn
  • A state's lane holds the events in that state and those it took part in; an actor's lane the events that name it (as participant, perpetrator, initiator or side). Events come from {% for k, m in meta.items() %}{{ m.name }} ({{ m.license }}, retrieved {{ m.retrieved }}){{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} – the list is on Events.
  • Library records: the actor's sanctions listings, terrorist designations, official attributions and the state's threat-level changes, each with its source. The thin row under a lane counts the library's reports naming the subject per year – each year opens that list.
  • The shaded background of a state's lane: deaths in organised violence per month (UCDP; the newest months are preliminary).
  • Chains: clicking an event draws the relations that a source states – Wikidata's has cause / has effect, immediate cause, follows / followed by, and relations written by hand with their evidence (sources/events.yaml, relations) – up to three steps back to its causes and forward to its effects, as arrows between the events on screen; the rest is dimmed. "Part of" is shown by highlighting the whole and its parts, not by arrows. Nothing is inferred: an event without a stated relation has no chain.
  • Weight: events are compared only with events of their own source – Wikipedia language editions with an article (Wikidata), weighted intensity (EuRepoC), deaths (UCDP). At each zoom a lane shows its heaviest events that fit; nothing is merged or estimated.
