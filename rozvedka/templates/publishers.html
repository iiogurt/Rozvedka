Think tanks and commercial analysts – credibility

Publishers that are not states: ◆ think tanks and ■ commercial analysts (companies that sell political-risk, security or threat analysis to clients). Some "think tanks" exist to influence politics for a state or an interest group, and a company answers to its owners and clients, so each one gets checks with evidence instead of a score – no current, credible ranking exists (the Go To index ended in 2020, Transparify in 2018). Think tanks: how openly they disclose who funds them, and whether autocratic governments do. Companies: who owns them, whether they name clients, authors and method. Both: US federal contracts and grants (USAspending.gov), official registers (EU Transparency Register, US FARA) and the sanctions lists of the US, UK, EU and UN. The badge colour shows the result everywhere a publisher appears; its owners, sponsors and founders are links of the network. Details: the design.
