Think tanks – credibility

Independent publishers are not run by states, and some "think tanks" exist to influence politics for a state or an interest group. No current, credible ranking of think tanks exists (the Go To index ended in 2020, Transparify in 2018), so each one gets checks with evidence instead of a score: how openly it discloses who funds it, whether autocratic governments fund it, official registers (EU Transparency Register, US FARA) and the sanctions lists of the US, UK, EU and UN. The badge colour shows the result everywhere a think tank appears. Details: the design.
