Entities whose stated links reach actors of several worlds – state services, cyber groups, terrorist groups, armed groups, organised crime: the facilitators, proxies and umbrellas between them. {{ pg.total|num }} entities.

How this is counted

For each entity of the Network, its neighbours in the stated layers (all but co-mentions in reports) that are actors of one of the five kinds are counted per kind. An entity reaching two kinds or more is listed, ranked by its links to the kind it reaches second most – a broker joins worlds, so one strong side is not enough. Each number opens exactly those links with their evidence.
