Notebooks

Passages you marked in reports and books, with your notes, in named notebooks. Each can be exported with a citation for every passage.

How this works

Mark a passage on a report's page in two ways: paste it under Quote in a notebook, or use ＋ notebook beside a passage the page shows. Pasted text is found in the library's text of the report, whatever its line breaks and case, and is kept as worded there, with its page. A passage the page shows is widened to its whole sentence. New passages go to the notebook marked current. A citation names the agency, the title, the year, the page of the file and the official address the report was downloaded from, with the date. Think tanks ◆, commercial analysts ■ and books are marked as such. There is one set of notebooks for the whole portal, kept in data/{{ file }}. That file is never committed and only personal backups carry it, because what you quote says what you are working on.
